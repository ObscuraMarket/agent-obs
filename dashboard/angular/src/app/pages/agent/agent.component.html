Obscura Agent

An AI trader that thinks out loud.

OBS is Obscura's own agent: it holds its own wallet, is built to route every swap through private liquidity and earns the same tokenized stock cashback as any trader. Its reasoning is published before each move and its book is marked to market every cycle. Every dollar of realised profit buys $OBS on the open market and is burned for good, with every burn on the explorer. The model decides what to say. Code decides what it can spend.
